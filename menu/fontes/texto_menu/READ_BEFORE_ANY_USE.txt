Brass Hopper is a serif font that embodies structural design.

A modern geometrical typeface, Brass Hopper is characterized by hard edges. The strict shapes of each letterform lends a sense of stability, making it ideal for art exhibitions and architectural projects. Its modern aesthetic merges with a touch of formality, creating a unique blend that stands out in any experimental context.

Brass Hopper is also available as a variable font, offering seamless adjustability in weight and style in hundreds of variations distilled down to a single font file. This allows for precise customization to match your design needs, providing flexibility and a wide range of typographic expressions within a single typeface.
More info about Variable Fonts: https://mansgreback.com/variable-fonts

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.

Designed by Mans Greback with a structural and experimental approach, Brass Hopper offers a distinctive style for those seeking a typeface that is both formal and innovative.
